Artisan Chronicles

The Craft of ZOHRA

The Warp of Benares

Ancestral Rhythms at the Pit Loom

Every heirloom drape begins on generational timber looms in Madanpura. Using the antique Kadwa technique, each gold leaf motif is individually locked into place, taking up to three months of focused human dedication.

"We weave not merely fabric, but the living memory of India's classical court traditions."

The Gilded Needle

Hallmarked Silver Zari & Beaten Dabka

Our embroidery uses real silver wire drawn into whisper-thin filaments and electroplated in 24k gold. Master karigars in Awadh affix each dabka spiral alongside delicate seed pearls to cultivate an authentic, enduring luster.

"True Zari never flashes harshly; it radiates with the gentle dignity of noble heirlooms."

The Modern Bride

Heirlooms Handed Across Generations

Designed with balanced drape weight and clean silhouettes, ZOHRA pieces move gracefully with the wearer. Every piece is woven to be preserved and cherished down through family generations.
